## Important distinctions

- 0756: The catalog also assigns 48624 to nonthreaded M2-624 on page 84. OEM number alone cannot distinguish these two designs. Preserve threaded-end wording and request physical thread/configuration confirmation; do not invent thread size, length or torque.
- 0888: The nearby guard-and-oil-tray stud is a different item, M-22 / 39122. Do not substitute it. A separate replacement manufacturer uses 6397790 for a similarly named item, but no verified supersession connects that identifier to PGE's part; do not add it as an alias.
- 0738: The catalog identifies a cover; it does not establish seals, fasteners, transparency, material, thickness, sterilization capability or mounting geometry.
