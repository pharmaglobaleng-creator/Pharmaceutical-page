## Verified facts and concise proposed additions

### PGE-MAN-0738 — inlet-bowl cover

Catalog p85 identifies Express inlet-bowl cover M2-669, reference 44669, in Product Control. This corroborates the existing PGE identity and category.

Suggested addition: “The inlet-bowl cover is a separately identified Express component. Match the installed cover and inlet-bowl arrangement before quotation; a photograph alone does not establish mounting geometry or material.”

The cover's sealing performance, dust containment, hygiene rating, attachment method and actual material are not verified. Replacement inspection should request existing-part measurements and mating-bowl information, without giving a fabricated procedure.

### PGE-MAN-0756 — threaded recirculating-channel pin

Catalog p86 assigns M-624 to the threaded variant; p84 assigns M2-624 to the unthreaded variant. Both carry 48624. This is the most useful part-specific distinction discovered.

Suggested addition: “Specify the threaded-end version when requesting PGE-MAN-0756. The published catalog also uses 48624 for an unthreaded pin, so the reference alone does not resolve the configuration. Supply the installed pin or its drawing for comparison.”

Assembly context is limited to the named recirculating channel and Product Control category. Do not claim the pin drives, meters, seals, precisely aligns or positively locks the channel without a drawing. No thread diameter, pitch, handedness, engagement length, fit, material, wear limit, torque or replacement schedule was verified. Asking for these details is appropriate; supplying invented values is not.

### PGE-MAN-0888 — guard stud

Catalog p96 identifies MK I guard stud M-779/39779. A different guard-and-oil-tray stud, M-22/39122, appears separately.

Suggested addition: “PGE-MAN-0888 identifies the guard stud. Keep the separate guard-and-oil-tray stud distinct when reviewing replacement hardware. Confirm the installed location and fastening details against the machine record.”

The designation provides guard-hardware context, but does not establish which guard is attached or the complete fastening arrangement. Do not invent thread size, stud length, strength class, coating, engagement, torque, safety certification or compatibility with other MK variants. Do not assert that this stud is part of an interlock or that replacing it certifies the guarding system.
