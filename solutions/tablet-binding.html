Frequently Asked Questions

Tablet Binding Solutions FAQ

What is tablet binding solutions?

It is a pharmaceutical compression or tooling condition that should be evaluated using the defect pattern, formulation, process, tooling, cleaning, equipment, and environmental evidence.

What commonly causes tablet binding solutions?

Possible causes include moisture, lubrication, binders, granulation, force, dwell time, speed, heat, tooling geometry, wear, corrosion, cleaning, and equipment condition.

Can photographs support a preliminary review?

Yes. Clear photographs help classify the defect and identify locations, but physical inspection or process data may still be required.

What process data should be collected?

Record force, precompression, dwell time, speed, feeder conditions, temperature, ejection behavior, run duration, and time-to-defect.

What formulation data should be collected?

Include moisture, binder, lubricant, glidant, granulation, particle-size distribution, fines, and batch history.

Can tooling condition contribute?

Yes. Wear, scratches, corrosion, geometry, engraving, dies, coatings, and alignment may all contribute.

Can polishing solve the problem?

Only when recoverable surface damage is part of the mechanism. Polishing does not correct every formulation or process cause.

Does every problem need a coating?

No. Coatings are one possible tool and should follow root-cause evaluation.

When should tooling be restored?

Restoration may be considered when damage is recoverable and critical dimensions, geometry, engraving, and integrity remain acceptable.

When should tooling be replaced?

Replacement may be necessary for cracks, severe dimensional loss, unsafe damage, or unrecoverable geometry.

How should corrective trials be performed?

Change one controlled variable at a time and document the baseline, modification, result, and repeatability.

Why is station mapping useful?

It helps separate local tooling or press conditions from formulation-wide or process-wide causes.

Does PharmaGlobalEng support worldwide projects?

Yes. Preliminary technical review and project coordination can be performed internationally, subject to scope, shipping, documentation, customs, and service availability.

Who approves return to production?

The manufacturer remains responsible for product, process, quality, validation, and return-to-service approval.

What is the best first step?

Define the defect precisely and collect representative evidence before selecting a corrective action.
