What causes tablet sticking and picking?

Tablet sticking and picking can result from interacting formulation, process, tooling, geometry, surface, coating, cleaning, and environmental variables. Sticking usually affects a broader punch-face area, while picking is localized around engraving, logos, score lines, character islands, and other recessed features. A strong investigation classifies the defect, documents when and where it appears, and evaluates the complete compression system before selecting corrective action.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tablet Sticking & Picking FAQ

What is tablet sticking?

Tablet sticking is adhesion across a broader punch-face area during compression. It can cause buildup, rough tablet surfaces, interrupted release, and production stops.

What is tablet picking?

Tablet picking is localized adhesion, often around lettering, logos, score lines, character islands, or recessed punch-face features.

What is the difference between sticking and picking?

Sticking usually affects a wider surface area, while picking is concentrated around local geometry.

Can punch polishing reduce sticking?

It may help when scratches, roughness, corrosion, or product-retention sites contribute, but it may not solve formulation or process causes.

Does every sticky formulation need a coating?

No. The better response may be formulation review, moisture control, lubrication adjustment, process changes, polishing, restoration, engraving optimization, or coating evaluation.

Which coating is best for sticking?

There is no universal best coating. CrN, DLC, TiN, TiCN, and other PVD coatings must be matched to the application.

Can moisture cause sticking?

Yes. Excess moisture, hygroscopic behavior, and humidity exposure can increase tackiness, but over-dry granulation can also affect release.

Can compression force cause sticking?

Force can influence contact stress, deformation, heat, and release behavior. The effect depends on the formulation.

Can press speed cause sticking?

Higher speed can change dwell time, heat, die fill, and the rate at which residue accumulates.

Can engraving cause picking?

Engraving depth, narrow spacing, islands, sharp internal corners, rough walls, and score lines can increase localized retention.

Can damaged coated punches be restored?

Possibly, after the coating, substrate, dimensions, edges, damage mechanism, and reprocessing route are evaluated.

What information should be submitted for evaluation?

Provide clear tooling and tablet photos, defect location, timing, tooling material and coating, cleaning methods, press conditions, formulation observations, and previous trials.

Can PharmaGlobalEng evaluate international projects?

Yes. Preliminary technical review and project coordination can be performed for manufacturers worldwide, subject to scope, shipping, documentation, customs, and service availability.

How often should tablet tooling be inspected?

Inspection frequency should reflect production volume, formulation abrasiveness, cleaning, coating history, prior defects, and risk.

When should punches be replaced?

Replacement may be required when cracks, severe dimensional loss, critical edge damage, unsafe structural problems, or unrecoverable geometry are present.
