Frequently Asked Questions

Tablet Sticking Solutions FAQ

What is tablet sticking solutions?

It is a pharmaceutical tooling and compression topic that should be evaluated within the complete formulation, process, tooling, cleaning, and operating system.

What causes problems related to tablet sticking solutions?

Possible causes include formulation behavior, moisture, lubrication, granulation, heat, force, dwell time, speed, geometry, wear, corrosion, coatings, cleaning, handling, and equipment condition.

Can photographs support a preliminary evaluation?

Yes. Clear photographs can support triage, but final recommendations may require physical inspection or additional process data.

What information should be submitted?

Include tooling and tablet photographs, defect location and timing, tooling material and coating, cleaning history, formulation observations, press conditions, and previous trials.

Does every problem require a coating?

No. The better response may involve formulation review, process adjustment, polishing, restoration, geometry review, cleaning changes, or replacement.

Can polishing solve every release problem?

No. Polishing may help when surface damage contributes, but moisture, chemistry, geometry, heat, and process conditions may remain primary causes.

How important is tooling geometry?

Cup depth, radii, lands, edges, engraving, logos, islands, and score lines can strongly influence compression and release.

How important is surface roughness?

Surface condition matters, but Ra alone does not describe all functional defects or predict production performance.

Can damaged coated tooling be restored?

Possibly, after coating identity, substrate, dimensions, geometry, damage, and reprocessing options are evaluated.

When should tooling be replaced?

Replacement may be necessary when cracks, severe dimensional loss, unsafe structural damage, or unrecoverable critical geometry are present.

How should trials be performed?

Change one controlled variable at a time and document the baseline, modification, result, and repeatability.

Can the same solution work for every formulation?

No. Different products can respond differently to the same surface, coating, geometry, and process.

Does PharmaGlobalEng support international manufacturers?

Yes. Preliminary review and project coordination can be performed worldwide, subject to scope, shipping, documentation, customs, and service availability.

Who approves return to production?

The manufacturer remains responsible for product, process, quality, validation, and return-to-service approval.

What is the first step?

Define the defect precisely and collect representative, traceable evidence before selecting a corrective action.
