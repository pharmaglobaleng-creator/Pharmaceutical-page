Tablet Picking Solutions

Problem Definition

What causes the issue: Tablet sticking is the adhesion of tablet material to the punch faces, leading to a poor-quality tablet surface and potential production stoppages.

Manufacturing impact: Sticking can cause tablet defects, reduce output, and increase tooling wear.

Engineering Analysis

Our engineering analysis of tablet sticking includes an evaluation of tooling factors, compression parameters, surface condition, and process variables.

Possible Solutions

  • Tooling inspection and refinement
  • Tooling changes to optimize geometry
  • Surface engineering to reduce adhesion
  • Process optimization to adjust compression parameters
