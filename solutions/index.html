Frequently Asked Questions

What tablet compression problems does PharmaGlobalEng solve?

PharmaGlobalEng provides engineering solutions for tablet sticking, picking, capping, lamination, chipping, binding, hardness variation, and tooling-related manufacturing issues.

How can tablet compression problems be reduced?

Compression problems can be reduced through evaluation of tooling condition, process parameters, surface characteristics, compression force, and manufacturing practices.

Why is tooling condition important for tablet quality?

Tablet tooling condition can influence tablet appearance, mechanical strength, defects, production consistency, and equipment performance.

How does compression optimization improve manufacturing?

Compression optimization helps improve tablet quality by evaluating process variables such as force, dwell time, tooling performance, and material behavior.
