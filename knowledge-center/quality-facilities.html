Quality Systems, Personnel, and Facilities

Source-linked engineering facts drawn from U.S. finished-pharmaceutical CGMP requirements for quality responsibilities, personnel, buildings, sanitation, and environmental controls.

These cards paraphrase selected requirements from 21 CFR Part 211. They are an engineering reference, not legal advice or a substitute for the current regulation, product application, or site quality system.
