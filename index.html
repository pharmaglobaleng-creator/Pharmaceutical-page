Tooling Life Extension

Not Every Punch Needs to Be Replaced

Many components removed from production because of corrosion, scratching, buildup, wear, or surface damage may still have usable life. Restoration can be considered when critical dimensions, geometry, engraving, structural integrity, and functional limits remain acceptable.

Pharmaceutical Tablet Tooling FAQs

Technical answers about tablet punch restoration, polishing, sticking, picking, coatings, engraving, surface engineering, inspection, and compression tooling support.

What is pharmaceutical tablet tooling?

Pharmaceutical tablet tooling includes punches and dies used in tablet presses to form, identify, and control compressed tablets.

What services does PharmaGlobalEng provide?

PharmaGlobalEng provides tablet punch restoration, precision polishing, surface engineering, coating evaluation, engraving optimization, tooling inspection, and compression-tooling support.

What causes tablet sticking during compression?

Tablet sticking may be influenced by formulation moisture, lubrication, tooling surface condition, punch geometry, heat, press speed, and compression settings.

What causes tablet picking?

Tablet picking is localized adhesion that often occurs in logos, lettering, score lines, or other recessed punch-face features.

What is the difference between tablet sticking and picking?

Sticking generally affects a broader punch-face area, while picking is localized around engraved or recessed features.

How can tablet sticking be reduced?

Solutions may include controlled polishing, tooling restoration, engraving refinement, surface engineering, coating evaluation, formulation review, or process adjustments.

Can worn tablet punches be restored?

Many punches can be restored when wear or damage remains within acceptable dimensional and functional limits.

When should tablet punches be replaced?

Replacement may be required when critical dimensions, geometry, structural integrity, engraving, or coating condition cannot be safely restored.

How does punch polishing improve tablet compression?

Controlled polishing can reduce surface irregularities and product-retention sites while supporting cleaner tablet release.

Can polishing damage tablet engraving?

Improper polishing can soften engraving edges or alter depth, so polishing should preserve critical tooling geometry.

What is pharmaceutical surface engineering?

It is the controlled optimization of tooling surfaces to influence release, friction, wear, corrosion resistance, cleanability, and consistency.

Can coatings help reduce tablet sticking?

Certain coatings may help in appropriate applications, but selection should be based on the root cause, formulation, tooling, and operating conditions.

What is chromium nitride coating?

Chromium nitride, or CrN, is a hard coating used in some tooling applications for wear, corrosion, and surface-performance benefits.

What is diamond-like carbon coating?

Diamond-like carbon, or DLC, is a family of low-friction, wear-resistant carbon-based coatings used in suitable tooling applications.

Which coating is best for sticky formulations?

There is no universal best coating; suitability depends on formulation chemistry, moisture, cleaning, tooling material, geometry, and process conditions.

How does engraving affect tablet picking?

Sharp internal corners, narrow spacing, rough walls, deep recesses, or worn engraving can increase product retention and picking risk.

Why do embossed tablets experience picking?

Material can remain inside recessed punch features and pull away from the tablet during release.

How often should tablet tooling be inspected?

Inspection frequency should reflect production volume, formulation abrasiveness, cleaning procedures, coating condition, and tooling history.

What signs show that tooling needs attention?

Warning signs include buildup, sticking, picking, corrosion, scratches, chipped edges, fading embossing, uneven wear, or poor tablet release.

Can damaged coated tooling be restored?

Some coated tooling can be restored depending on the coating, damage, substrate condition, remaining dimensions, and recoating requirements.

Does every sticky formulation need a coating?

No. Some issues are better addressed through polishing, engraving refinement, restoration, moisture control, lubrication, or process changes.

Does PharmaGlobalEng serve international manufacturers?

Yes. PharmaGlobalEng supports pharmaceutical manufacturers in the United States and international markets.

What should be included with a tooling inquiry?

Include tooling photos, tablet images, tooling type, defect location, coating details, cleaning method, process conditions, and the desired outcome.

Can PharmaGlobalEng evaluate a problem using photographs?

Photographs can support a preliminary review, although final recommendations may require physical inspection or additional process information.

How do customers request a tooling evaluation?

Customers can submit contact details, tooling information, photographs, defect descriptions, and technical objectives for review.
