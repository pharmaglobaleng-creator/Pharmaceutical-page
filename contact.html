Make your inquiry useful

A few details help us get started

You do not need to have every answer. Send what you know and tell us which details still need to be confirmed.

For a replacement-part request

  • Your contact details: name, company, preferred reply address and phone number.
  • Equipment identification: machine manufacturer, model and serial number or configuration, if available.
  • Part identification: description, OEM or PGE number, and clear photographs or an approved drawing. Mark any uncertain reference as unconfirmed.
  • Quotation requirements: quantity, delivery location and requested timing. Let us know about any critical dimensions or material requirements.

Missing a part number? Use our part identification page to see what information to provide.

For a coating or tooling inquiry

  • The tooling: component type, quantity, substrate material and existing coating or finish, if known.
  • The concern: wear, damage, sticking, picking or another observed condition, and where it appears.
  • Supporting information: clear photographs, relevant measurements, cleaning methods and service history.
  • Your objective: inspection, restoration, coating evaluation or troubleshooting, along with the specifications and quality requirements that apply.

Coating selection and any restoration recommendation depend on the application and the condition of the tooling.

Before sending tooling or samples

Contact PharmaGlobalEng first to agree on scope, handling, documentation, shipping, cleaning status, and any site-specific quality requirements. Do not send active ingredients, product residue, confidential product data, or controlled materials without prior written coordination. Share only information your organization has authorized you to provide.
