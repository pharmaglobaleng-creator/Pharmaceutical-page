Tooling Inspection & Maintenance Checklist

Purpose and scope

Provide a traceable inspection and disposition record for tablet-compression punches and dies before release, during scheduled maintenance and after a defect investigation. Use with the applicable OEM instructions and approved site procedures.

Preparation and responsibilities

  1. Authorized personnel isolate the press and remove tooling according to the site procedure. Preserve suspect tools and evidence from affected stations.
  2. Record condition before cleaning. Clean and dry using the approved, coating-compatible process; do not inspect through product residue.
  3. Confirm drawings, calibrated instrument IDs and applicable acceptance criteria. If a limit or method is missing, hold the tool for Engineering review.
  4. Inspect visually and dimensionally using the table below. Record actual values, not just a pass mark.

Disposition and release

Polish: only an approved surface treatment that leaves geometry and coating within specification. Restore: a qualified repair, with approved final dimensions and documented reinspection. Replace: remove from service when damage cannot be acceptably restored. Inspection does not require polishing if the tool is already acceptable.

  1. Segregate suspect tools. Notify Quality of cracks, flaking, metal loss or other potential product-impact findings; assess affected production under the site process.
  2. Record the defect, proposed action, responsible person and work order. Never return a suspect tool solely because it looks better after polishing.
  3. After authorized maintenance, repeat affected measurements, confirm set compatibility and record the release decision.
  4. Preserve and store accepted tools by the approved method. Retain photographs, instrument records, measurements and history under the site retention policy.
