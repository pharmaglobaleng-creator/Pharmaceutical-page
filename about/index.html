Our Story

More Than Five Decades of Pharmaceutical Tooling Experience

Our legacy is not simply how long we have been in business. It is what our family has spent that time learning, improving, and solving.

The PharmaGlobalEng story began with a direct manufacturing challenge. Tablet sticking and picking can reduce tablet quality, interrupt production, damage embossed detail, increase cleaning frequency, and make an otherwise capable tablet press difficult to operate consistently. Our founder recognized that the solution could not be reduced to a single generic answer. Tooling condition, punch-face geometry, surface finish, engraving, coatings, formulation behavior, cleaning, heat, moisture, and compression conditions can all influence release behavior.

That early focus on solving sticking and picking became the foundation of our engineering philosophy: identify the real mechanism, understand the tooling, correct the surface or component condition when appropriate, and match the solution to the application rather than forcing every problem into the same treatment.

Over the decades, that approach expanded into a broader pharmaceutical tooling and component business. Today, PharmaGlobalEng supports customers through tablet tooling restoration, precision polishing, surface engineering, coating evaluation, tooling inspection, failure analysis, lifecycle support, tablet press troubleshooting, replacement parts, and technical problem solving.

Where It Started

The Sticking & Picking Problem That Shaped Our Company

A manufacturing problem with no room for shortcuts.

When powder or granulation adheres to a tablet punch face, the problem may appear as broad sticking or as localized picking in lettering, logos, score lines, islands, or recessed features. The visible defect is only the symptom. The real cause may involve the surface, geometry, coating, material, formulation, press conditions, cleaning process, or a combination of factors.

Our founder’s work centered on developing a practical solution to that problem—an approach that became part of the company’s identity and the beginning of our long-term focus on tooling surfaces and tablet release.

“Do not treat the symptom alone. Understand why the tablet is sticking, then engineer the tooling and surface around the real cause.”

That philosophy still guides our work today.

We do not believe every sticking problem needs the same coating, every worn punch should automatically be replaced, or every performance issue comes from the tooling. The strongest solution starts with evidence.

Surface finish and wear pattern
