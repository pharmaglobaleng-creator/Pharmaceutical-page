Show the formulas and assumptions

Monthly stop hours = batches × stops per batch × minutes per stop ÷ 60. Avoidable stop labor = hours × people × loaded hourly cost × avoidable labor %. Stop-linked cost pool = avoidable stop labor + cleaning supplies + avoidable machine costs.

Other cost pool = investigation + rework + polishing + (replacement cost × annual replacement events ÷ 12). Monthly savings = stop-linked pool × stop reduction + reject cost × reject reduction + other pool × other reduction. Monthly net cash benefit = savings − added recurring cost.

Annual net benefit = monthly net × 12. First-year net subtracts the one-time investment. Simple payback = investment ÷ positive monthly net benefit. Twelve-month break-even stop reduction = (investment ÷ selected target months + recurring cost − reject savings − other savings) ÷ stop-linked cost pool. A negative threshold is shown as 0%; a threshold above 100% is not achievable from stops alone under these assumptions.

Capacity value = recovered stop hours × user-entered incremental contribution per hour, shown separately. No tax, financing, discounting or delayed ramp-up is modeled. Include implementation downtime and qualification costs in the one-time cost if applicable. Constant volume, stop duration and proportional cost reductions are assumed.

A fully worked fictional example

In USD, 20 batches × 2 stops × 30 minutes gives 20 stopped hours a month. Two people at $40/hour allocate $1,600 of labor; at 50% avoidability, $800 enters the cash model. Cleaning adds $200, making the stop-linked pool $1,000/month. Rejects cost $400/month. Investigation, rework, polishing and replacement add $350/month.

At 50% fewer stops, 25% fewer rejects and 20% lower other costs, gross savings are $500 + $100 + $70 = $670/month. Subtract $100 recurring cost: $570/month or $6,840/year. A $4,000 investment leaves $2,840 first-year net benefit and a 7.0-month simple payback. Twelve-month break-even requires 26.33% fewer stops, holding the other assumptions constant. The low/high scenarios yield $3,120/$10,560 annual net benefit. These are illustrative arithmetic inputs, not customer outcomes.
