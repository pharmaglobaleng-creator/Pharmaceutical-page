Tablet sticking and picking: evidence matrix

PharmaGlobalEng · Version 1.2 · October 17, 2026

From tablet defect to evidence

Use this matrix to organize observations for a qualified review. Follow your approved isolation, cleaning and inspection procedures.

Observation → evidence to collect → review question
What you observeWhat to recordWhat to investigate
Powder on the punch facePhotograph the deposit before cleaning; record affected stations, onset and batch.Review tooling condition, formulation and process history together. A coating is one possible route, not an automatic answer.
Powder collecting in logos or numbersCapture a close-up of the engraving and matching tablet defect; identify tool and station.Review engraving geometry, finish and formulation interaction. Do not assume polishing alone resolves it.
Defect returns after cleaningRecord time or tablets produced until recurrence and cleaning method.Compare repeat intervals under comparable conditions; distinguish temporary deposit removal from a sustained change.
Only some stations are affectedMap defects to tool IDs and stations; retain photographs and inspection records.Check for tool-to-tool differences using the approved inspection procedure.
Defects change between batchesRecord lot, sampling method, environment and relevant process records.Review the product/process differences before attributing the problem to tooling.
Visible tool damage or coating lossRecord condition and segregate suspect tooling under site procedures.Obtain qualified inspection before considering reuse, restoration or a trial.
