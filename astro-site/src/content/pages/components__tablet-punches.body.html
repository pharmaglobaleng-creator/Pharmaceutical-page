Identify upper and lower punches correctly

Keep the tooling set reference, individual tool marking and upper or lower position with the inspection record. Include the press manufacturer and model and the drawing revision. For shaped tools, identify orientation and keying features. A photograph can assist identification, but it cannot replace the specified dimensions and interfaces.

If an old tool differs from the drawing, document both rather than assuming the fitted item is correct. It may reflect previous service or a different revision. Resolve that uncertainty before copying, purchasing or modifying a replacement. Preserve the identity of any mating die used during the affected run.

Review the cup, engraving and tip edge

Examine the tablet-contact region for deposits, visible damage and changes at the cup perimeter or engraved detail. Compare the corresponding tablet feature with the oriented tool photograph. A defect that follows a letter creates a different inspection question from broad face buildup or damage at the tip edge.

Clean only through the approved procedure and distinguish the as-found condition from the cleaned inspection. Uncontrolled polishing may round a feature or change the tablet design. Use the drawing to determine which features need measurement or specialist examination; visual improvement alone does not demonstrate conformity.

Include the barrel and head in the assessment

A complaint about a tablet face should not limit inspection to the cup. Record the condition of the barrel and head and the location of any marks. Compare those observations with the press and service history. Similar marks across a set and an isolated mark at one station should be recorded separately.

Keep tooling lubrication questions distinct from formulation lubrication questions. The press maintenance instructions govern machine interfaces and approved maintenance. Do not transfer a general lubricant recommendation to a product-contact surface. Where the tool condition suggests an unresolved machine interaction, assess that relationship before installing a replacement.

Measure the features that govern the decision

Working length, overall length and cup depth are different inspection quantities. Identify the dimension being measured and its reference surfaces. Use a suitable method and the applicable acceptance limits; do not substitute a convenient overall dimension for a working dimension specified by the drawing.

Record individual results against tool identity, then assess the set as required by the tooling specification. Preserve previous measurements where available to distinguish a new finding from a longstanding difference. A suspected crack, deformation or missing material needs a separate disposition rather than a cosmetic repair.

Choose restoration or replacement with the drawing in view

Restoration is an option only when its defined scope can leave the tool suitable for the intended application. Ask which surfaces will change, what material may be removed, which dimensions will be checked afterward and what conditions would cause the work to stop. Coating or recoating also needs a confirmed substrate and remaining geometry.

For replacement, provide the approved specification, required quantity and set requirements, not just the damaged sample. Ask PharmaGlobalEng to confirm the proposed scope, compatibility and availability. Retain the inspection and disposition record so future failures can be compared with an identified baseline.

Questions and answers

Can upper and lower tablet punches be interchanged?

Do not assume interchangeability. Their design and function must match the approved tooling and press specification.

Is overall length the same as working length?

No. They describe different measurements. Use the correct reference surfaces and dimension defined by the tooling specification.

Can polishing change a tablet logo?

Uncontrolled removal around engraving can alter its geometry. Any restoration must preserve the approved feature requirements.

Does a punch need inspection if its cup looks good?

Yes. The barrel, head, dimensions and integrity can matter even when the cup appears acceptable.

What should I send for a replacement punch enquiry?

Provide the machine identity, drawing revision, upper or lower position, tool markings, set reference, quantity and photographs.

Can a cracked punch be returned after coating?

A coating is not evidence of restored structural integrity. Isolate the tool and obtain an appropriate disposition.
