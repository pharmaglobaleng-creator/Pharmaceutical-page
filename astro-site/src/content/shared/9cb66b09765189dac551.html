Tooling Corrosion and Wear: Compare the Evidence

Updated September 20, 2026

Corrosion and wear can occur on the same tablet tool, but they ask different diagnostic questions. Corrosion involves chemical or electrochemical attack; wear involves material loss or surface change through mechanical interaction. A stain, rough patch or tablet defect alone does not identify either mechanism. Start by comparing the damage location, service history and condition before and after approved cleaning.
