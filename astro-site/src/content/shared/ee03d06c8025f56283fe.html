Tablet Dies: Bore Condition, Tooling Fit and Replacement Evidence

Updated September 20, 2026

A tablet die defines the cavity that works with the punches during filling and compression. Its bore also forms part of the tablet’s ejection path. Evaluating a die therefore requires more than checking its outside appearance: identify the bore, mating tooling, installed orientation where applicable and the controlling drawing.
