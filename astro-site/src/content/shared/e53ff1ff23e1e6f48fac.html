Titanium Carbonitride (TiCN): Evaluate the Wear Question First

Updated September 20, 2026

Titanium carbonitride, abbreviated TiCN, is a coating material used in engineered wear-resistant surface systems. For tablet tooling, begin with the wear mechanism you are trying to address. A hardness figure or the TiCN name alone does not show that a particular coated tool will retain its geometry or release a specific formulation acceptably.
