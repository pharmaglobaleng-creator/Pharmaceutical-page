Pharmaceutical Tablet Tooling Inspection Services

What it is: A comprehensive evaluation of tablet tooling to assess its condition and identify potential issues.

Who needs it: Pharmaceutical and nutraceutical manufacturers who want to ensure tooling reliability and tablet quality.

Why it matters: Proactive inspection helps prevent costly downtime, reduces tablet defects, and extends tooling life.

Engineering Overview

Our engineering overview includes a thorough evaluation of your tooling, including dimensional inspection, surface condition analysis, and wear analysis to understand its impact on manufacturing.

Common Problems Identified

  • Tablet sticking
  • Picking
  • Weight variation
  • Premature tooling failure
  • Compression issues

Inspection Process

  1. Application review
  2. Tooling evaluation
  3. Condition assessment
  4. Engineering recommendations
  5. Documentation

Benefits

  • Reduced downtime
  • Improved tablet quality
  • Extended tooling life
