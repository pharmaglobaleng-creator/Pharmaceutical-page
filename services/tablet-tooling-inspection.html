Frequently Asked Questions

What pharmaceutical tooling services does PharmaGlobalEng provide?

PharmaGlobalEng provides tablet tooling inspection, failure analysis, lifecycle management, restoration, surface engineering, coatings, maintenance, and manufacturing support services.

Why is tablet tooling inspection important?

Tooling inspection helps identify wear, damage, dimensional changes, and potential manufacturing issues before they impact production performance.

How does tooling lifecycle management improve manufacturing?

Lifecycle management helps organizations plan maintenance, evaluate tooling condition, improve reliability, and support consistent tablet production.

What is the relationship between tooling and tablet quality?

Tablet tooling condition can influence compression behavior, defects, tablet appearance, and manufacturing consistency.
