Tablet Punch Restoration Services

What it is: A service to restore worn or damaged tablet punches to a usable condition.

Who needs it: Manufacturers looking to extend the life of their tooling and reduce replacement costs.

Why it matters: Restoration can be a cost-effective alternative to purchasing new tooling, without compromising quality.

Engineering Overview

Our engineering overview includes a thorough evaluation of your tooling, including dimensional inspection, surface condition analysis, and wear analysis to understand its impact on manufacturing.

Common Problems Identified

  • Tablet sticking
  • Picking
  • Weight variation
  • Premature tooling failure
  • Compression issues

Inspection Process

  1. Application review
  2. Tooling evaluation
  3. Condition assessment
  4. Engineering recommendations
  5. Documentation

Benefits

  • Improved tooling reliability
  • Reduced downtime
  • Improved tablet quality
  • Extended tooling life
