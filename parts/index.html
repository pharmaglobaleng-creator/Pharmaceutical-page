Frequently asked questions

Tablet Press Parts Sourcing

How do I find the right part for my tablet press?

Start with the manufacturer and machine model, then narrow by component family. Provide serial information, reference numbers, dimensions, drawings, or photographs when available so compatibility can be reviewed.

Can I request a part when I do not know the part number?

Yes. Submit the machine manufacturer, model, component location, description, and available images or drawings through the parts inquiry form.

Why is compatibility reviewed before checkout?

Tablet press components can vary by machine configuration and revision. Compatibility review reduces the risk of ordering an incorrect component.

Are prices shown publicly?

Pricing is provided after compatibility, availability, lead time, and quotation details are reviewed.
