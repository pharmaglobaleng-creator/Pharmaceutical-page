Questions

Korsch 300 parts ordering

Why are prices not displayed?

Pricing depends on the exact part configuration, material, quantity, availability, manufacturing route, documentation, shipping destination, and lead time. A price is issued after those details are reviewed.

What does the checkout code do?

The code identifies an approved quotation. It should be unique, expire after a limited period, and unlock only the reviewed cart, price, shipping terms, and secure payment session associated with that quotation.

Can I request a part that is not listed?

Yes. Send the machine model, component name, existing reference, photographs, drawings, measurements, material, and quantity through the parts inquiry process.

Does listing a part guarantee compatibility or stock?

No. The page identifies part types that may be evaluated. Compatibility, supply, stock, pricing, and lead time are confirmed only in the approved quotation.
