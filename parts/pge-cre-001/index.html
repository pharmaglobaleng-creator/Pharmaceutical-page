What this part does

The channel restrictor plate is used in the product-handling path of the Cremer CF-1220 to help keep product flow controlled and separated as tablets or capsules move toward the counting and filling section. The evenly spaced channels limit side-to-side movement and help maintain a more predictable path through the machine. This can support smoother feeding, more stable spacing, and more consistent presentation of product before it reaches downstream counting or discharge components.

Why the plate matters

Different products can behave differently depending on tablet diameter, capsule size, coating, shape, weight, and surface condition. A channel restrictor plate helps guide these products through defined lanes so that product does not wander freely across the feed path. Maintaining this separation can reduce irregular movement, bunching, bridging, or uneven distribution that may interfere with reliable machine operation.

Inspection and maintenance

Routine inspection should include the channel walls, openings, mounting areas, and all product-contact surfaces. Look for cracks, chips, deformation, rough edges, staining, buildup, or dimensional changes that could affect product movement. A damaged or distorted plate can change the way tablets or capsules enter and leave the channels. The component should remain clean, smooth, and properly aligned during production and changeover.

Fit and compatibility

Before replacement, verify the exact channel count, overall width, channel spacing, channel depth, mounting features, material, and installed machine configuration. Cremer CF-1220 machines can be configured differently depending on the product and application, so visual similarity alone is not enough to confirm fit. PharmaGlobalEng reviews the existing part, drawing information, dimensions, or machine details before manufacturing or quoting a replacement.

Application notes

This plate is intended to support controlled product flow in Cremer counting equipment. Its geometry helps keep individual product streams separated before counting and filling. Proper condition and alignment can contribute to smooth feeding and consistent machine performance, while worn or damaged features may lead to product hang-ups or uneven flow.

Replacement guidance

When ordering a replacement, provide the machine model, serial number if available, a clear photograph of the installed component, critical measurements, and any drawing or part references.
