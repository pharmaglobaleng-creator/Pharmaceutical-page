Tablet tooling questions

What tablet tooling services does PharmaGlobalEng provide?

Our services include tooling inspection, punch polishing, restoration assessment, engraving review and coating support. Send your tooling details so we can confirm the appropriate scope.

Can you help with tablet punches and dies already in use?

Yes. We can review existing punches and dies against the reported problem, their condition and available dimensional records. Photos support an initial discussion; final recommendations may require physical inspection.

Can a coating reduce tablet sticking or picking?

A suitable coating may help when the working surface contributes to adhesion. Formulation, tooling geometry and operating conditions also matter. We supply CrN and DLC coatings, with suitability considered for the application.

When is replacement more appropriate than restoration?

Cracks, unacceptable dimensional loss or unrecoverable damage to critical features may rule out restoration. The decision should follow inspection against the required specification.

What should I send for a tablet tooling inquiry?

Send the press make and model, punch and die details, quantity, photographs, available drawings or measurements, coating history and the problem or work you need addressed. Confirm handling and shipping arrangements before sending tools.

How much does tablet tooling work cost and how long does it take?

Pricing and turnaround depend on the quantity, condition, inspection requirements and agreed work. Contact PharmaGlobalEng for a project-specific quotation and schedule before planning a production shutdown.
